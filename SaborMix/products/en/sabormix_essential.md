# PRODUCT SHEET: SaborMix Essential

## SaborMix Essential
**The ultimate kitchen assistant that transforms your daily routine.**

### Description
Discover a new way to enjoy cooking with **SaborMix Essential**. Designed to simplify your life, this revolutionary all-in-one kitchen robot replaces more than 12 traditional appliances. Whether you are a beginner cook or an experienced chef, SaborMix Essential lets you prepare delicious, healthy and quick meals with just the press of a button. Save time, space and effort while impressing your loved ones with perfect textures and intense flavours. Haute cuisine has never been so accessible!

### Technical Specifications
*   **Motor:** 500W (nominal power) / 1000W (maximum processing power).
*   **Heating system:** Integrated 1000W resistance element.
*   **Bowl capacity:** 2.2 litres (Food-grade stainless steel).
*   **Temperature range:** 37°C to 120°C (adjustable in 5°C intervals).
*   **Speeds:** 10 speed levels + Turbo mode.
*   **Display:** High-contrast monochrome LCD display with capacitive touch buttons.
*   **Integrated scale:** 5-gram precision.
*   **Dimensions:** 32 cm (Width) x 32 cm (Depth) x 30 cm (Height).
*   **Weight:** 7.5 kg.

### Main Features
SaborMix Essential masters over 12 culinary techniques:
1.  **Mix and Blend:** Ideal for smoothies, creams and sauces.
2.  **Chop and Grind:** From ice to nuts or vegetables in seconds.
3.  **Knead:** Achieve bread or pizza dough with a professional texture thanks to the spike mode.
4.  **Steam:** Preserve all nutrients using the internal steaming basket.
5.  **Emulsify:** Perfect mayonnaises and vinaigrettes that never split.
6.  **Reverse rotation:** Stir delicate stews without breaking the ingredients.
7.  **Weigh:** Weigh directly in the bowl as you add ingredients.

### User Manual (Quick Guide)
1.  **Assembly:** Place the bowl on the base until you hear the safety "click".
2.  **Ingredients:** Add the ingredients using the integrated scale as a guide.
3.  **Settings:** Use the front buttons to set **Time** (up to 60 min), **Temperature** (37°-120°) and **Speed** (1-10).
4.  **Start:** Press the central "START" button.
5.  **Cleaning:** After use, add water, a drop of washing-up liquid and use Speed 8 for 10 seconds for a quick self-clean.
