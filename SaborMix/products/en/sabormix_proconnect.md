# PRODUCT SHEET: SaborMix ProConnect

## SaborMix ProConnect
**The future of haute cuisine, connected to your home.**

### Description
Take your culinary experience to the next level with **SaborMix ProConnect**. This is not just a kitchen robot; it is your intelligent personal chef. Thanks to its built-in Wi-Fi connectivity and large full-color touchscreen, you will have access to an endless universe of step-by-step guided recipes. Not at home? Control, monitor and plan your menus from your smartphone with the SaborMix app. Extreme power, high temperatures for searing meat, and connectivity that will make your life easier. Cooking like a Michelin star is now intelligent, intuitive and effortless.

### Technical Specifications
*   **Motor:** 1500W magnetic reluctance motor (Ultra silent).
*   **Heating system:** 1500W smart induction.
*   **Bowl capacity:** 3.0 litres (Premium stainless steel, ideal for families).
*   **Temperature range:** 37°C to **160°C** (Allows searing, high-temperature sauteing and caramelizing).
*   **Speeds:** 12 speed levels + Turbo mode + Spoon mode (ultra slow).
*   **Display:** 8-inch full-color capacitive TFT touchscreen.
*   **Integrated scale:** High precision gram by gram (1g).
*   **Connectivity:** Wi-Fi (2.4/5GHz) and Bluetooth 5.0. Native app for iOS and Android.
*   **Dimensions:** 34 cm (Width) x 34 cm (Depth) x 33 cm (Height).
*   **Weight:** 8.9 kg.

### Premium Features (Additional to Essential model)
In addition to the 12 basic functions, the ProConnect includes:
1.  **Guided Cooking (SmartCooking):** The screen shows you step by step which ingredient to add. It automatically sets time, temperature and speed for you.
2.  **Mobile App Sync:** Send the recipe from your phone to the SaborMix, receive notifications when the food is ready and create automatic shopping lists.
3.  **High Temperature (160°C):** Saute onions until golden or sear meat as in a frying pan.
4.  **Slow Cooking and Sous-Vide:** Cook sous-vide at low temperature for up to 12 hours for melt-in-the-mouth meats.
5.  **Fermentation:** Maintain stable temperatures (37°-40°) to make homemade yogurts or prove doughs like an expert.
6.  **OTA Updates (Over The Air):** Your machine receives new features and cooking modes via Wi-Fi for free.

### User Manual (Quick Smart Guide)
1.  **Power on and connect:** Switch on the device and follow the assistant on the 8" screen to connect it to your home Wi-Fi network.
2.  **Mobile pairing:** Download the *SaborMix* app and scan the QR code on the screen to link your account.
3.  **Choose a recipe:** Browse the catalogue on the screen or on your phone. Select "Cook Today".
4.  **Guided cooking:** The machine will weigh exactly what you need (thanks to the 1g scale) and tell you when to press "Next". It adjusts the cooking parameters by itself.
5.  **Maintenance:** Select the "Smart Cleaning" menu on the screen. The machine will heat the water and remove food residue (including caramel) automatically.
