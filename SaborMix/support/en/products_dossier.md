# Product Dossier

**Document objective:** Provide information on pricing, financing methods, shipping and return policies for the SaborMix Sales Agent.

### 1. Product Pricing
*   **SaborMix Essential:** €599.00 (VAT included).
*   **SaborMix ProConnect:** €1,199.00 (VAT included).
*   **SaborMix Cloud Subscription (Recipes):** 1st year free with the ProConnect model. After that, €39.00/year or €3.99/month. (The Essential model does not include Wi-Fi, so it does not require this subscription; it uses a physical recipe book).

### 2. Accepted Payment Methods
*   **Credit/Debit Cards:** Visa, Mastercard and American Express.
*   **Digital Wallets:** PayPal, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
*   **Financing (Instalment payments):**
    *   Through our financial partner (Klarna/Cetelem).
    *   Can be financed over **12, 24 or 36 months with no interest (0% APR)**.
    *   *Example for the ProConnect over 24 months:* €49.95/month.
*   **Bank Transfer:** Shipment will be processed once the funds are received in the SaborMix account (usually takes an extra 24-48 working hours).

### 3. Shipping Times and Costs
*   **Standard Shipping (Mainland Spain and Balearic Islands):** FREE. Delivery in 24-48 working hours.
*   **Express Shipping (Mainland Spain):** €9.99. If ordered before 14:00, guaranteed delivery before 10:00 the following day.
*   **Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla:** Shipping cost of €29.00. Delivery in 3-5 working days (mainland VAT is deducted; the customer assumes local taxes and customs on receipt).

### 4. Returns Policy and Legal Warranty
*   **30-day trial:** The customer has 30 calendar days from receipt to try the SaborMix. If not satisfied, we collect the machine free of charge and refund 100% of the payment (the machine must be returned clean and in its original packaging).
*   **Legal Warranty:** All SaborMix products come with a **3-year legal warranty** against manufacturing defects.
