# Troubleshooting
**Document objective:** Provide step-by-step solutions for the most common failures, initial setup and repair management for the Support Agent.

### 1. Setup and Connectivity Issues (ProConnect only)
**Issue: "I cannot connect the SaborMix to Wi-Fi" or "The app does not sync".**
*   **Solution 1 (2.4GHz network):** The SaborMix ProConnect needs to be connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network. Ask the customer to check their router to ensure they are not trying to connect exclusively to the 5GHz network.
*   **Solution 2 (Network reset):** On the SaborMix screen, go to *Settings > Connectivity > Forget Network*. Restart the home router, wait 2 minutes and reconnect.
*   **Solution 3 (App QR code):** If the mobile app does not sync the recipe, ask the customer to log out of the app, log back in and scan the QR code again that appears in *Settings > Link Device* on the machine screen.

### 2. Motor and Blade Issues
**Issue: "The blades do not spin", "It is making a strange noise" or "Error E-03 appears".**
*   **Common cause (Error E-03 - Overload):** There is too much food, the dough is too dense (e.g. bread) or a hard food (a bone) has jammed the blade.
*   **Solution:**
    1. Turn off the machine immediately.
    2. Remove half of the dough/food from the bowl.
    3. Manually check whether anything is blocking the blades.
    4. Allow the motor to cool for 15 minutes (for thermal safety) and turn it back on.
*   **Issue: "The bowl does not fit / The machine beeps and will not start".**
    *   **Solution:** Check that the golden pins at the base of the bowl are completely clean and dry. If the bowl does not make the magnetic safety "click", the machine will not allow the blades to spin.

### 3. Heating System Issues
**Issue: "The food is not heating up", "Error E-05 appears" or "The bottom has burnt".**
*   **Common cause (Error E-05 - Thermal contact failure):** The connection pins under the bowl are wet (this usually happens after washing the bowl).
    *   **Solution:** Dry the outer bottom of the bowl (where the metal connectors are) and the machine base recess very thoroughly with a cloth.
*   **Issue: Resistance failure (Not heating at all despite being dry).**
    *   **Agent action:** If the machine emits no heat at all after ruling out moisture, the safety thermostat or induction element may have blown. This should be referred to **Specialised Technical Support (SAT) for warranty repair**.

### 4. Software Freeze / Frozen Screen
**Issue: "The ProConnect touchscreen is not responding" or "It has frozen".**
*   **Solution (Hard Reset):** Hold down the central physical power button for **15 seconds** until the screen turns off completely. Unplug the machine from the wall for 1 minute, plug it back in and turn it on.

### 5. SAT Referral Protocol (Technical Assistance Service)
If the previous steps do not resolve the issue and the unit is broken, the Agent must initiate this protocol:
1.  Ask the customer for the **Serial Number** (found on a sticker underneath the machine or in *Settings > About*).
2.  Ask for a brief description of the problem and the collection address.
3.  **Process:** SaborMix will send a courier to the customer's address with a special protective box (within 24-48h). The customer only needs to place the defective machine (without accessories) in the box. The estimated repair time at our workshops is **5 to 7 working days**. The cost is €0 if under warranty.
