# Traditional Steamed Coffee Flan
**Difficulty:** Low | **Prep time:** 5 min | **Total time:** 50 min + chilling | **Compatible with:** Essential and ProConnect.

*A foolproof and silky dessert. We will cook the flan using steam, taking advantage of the constant moisture of the XXL external steamer of your SaborMix so it has no bubbles.*

### Ingredients
*   4 large eggs (size L).
*   370g condensed milk (1 small tin).
*   400g whole milk.
*   2 tablespoons instant coffee (or 50ml very strong espresso).
*   Liquid caramel for the base of the mould.
*   1000g (1 litre) of water to generate steam.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1.  **Prepare the mould:** Coat the bottom and sides of an aluminium or silicone mould (one that fits inside the steamer) with the liquid caramel.
2.  **The flan mixture:** Add the 4 eggs, condensed milk, whole milk and coffee to the SaborMix bowl. Place the lid.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **30 seconds / Speed 4.** (We do not use a higher speed to avoid creating too much foam).
3.  **Fill the mould:** Pour the mixture into the caramel-lined mould. Cover it tightly with aluminium foil (make sure it is airtight so no steam water gets in) and place some sheets of absorbent paper on top in case condensation forms.
4.  **Prepare the steam:** Without washing the bowl, add the 1000g of water. Close the bowl without the measuring cap and place the **XXL Steamer** (or the basket if using very small moulds) on top with the mould inside. Cover the steamer.
5.  **Steam cooking:**
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **45 minutes / Temperature 120°C (or Steam function) / Speed 2.**
6.  **Chilling:** When done, remove the mould carefully to avoid burns. Allow it to cool to room temperature, then place it in the fridge for a minimum of 4 hours before unmoulding.
