# Duck and Sausage Cassoulet (Quick Style)
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Prep time:** 10 min | **Total time:** 45 min | **Compatible with:** Essential and ProConnect.

*We have adapted this classic and laborious French stew so that your SaborMix does the hard work. We will use "Reverse Rotation" so that the beans and meat are not shredded during cooking.*

### Ingredients
*   800g tinned white beans (rinsed and well drained).
*   2 duck confit legs (shredded and deboned).
*   4 fresh pork sausages (Toulouse style or butcher's), cut into 3 pieces each.
*   150g smoked bacon lardons.
*   1 large onion (quartered).
*   2 garlic cloves and 2 carrots (thickly sliced).
*   400g chicken or meat stock.
*   2 tablespoons tomato paste.
*   1 *Bouquet garni* (1 bay leaf and a sprig of thyme tied together).

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1.  **Chop the soffritto:** Place the onion, garlic and carrots in the bowl.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **5 seconds / Speed 5.** Use the spatula to scrape the sides of the bowl down towards the blades.
2.  **Saute and brown the bacon:** Add the smoked bacon.
    *   *If you have the ProConnect:* Take advantage of the **High Temperature** mode. Set **8 minutes / 160°C / Spoon speed**. (It will be beautifully golden!).
    *   *If you have the Essential:* Set **8 minutes / 120°C / Speed 1**.
3.  **Seal the sausages:** Add the sausage pieces to the bowl. From this point it is VITAL to use reverse rotation to avoid cutting the meat.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* Activate **Reverse Rotation**. Set **5 minutes / 120°C / Spoon speed** (the slowest speed).
4.  **Cook the stew:** Add the chicken stock, tomato paste, shredded duck confit and the *bouquet garni*.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* Keep **Reverse Rotation** active. Set **15 minutes / 100°C / Spoon speed**.
5.  **Add the beans:** A few minutes to go. Carefully add the drained white beans to the bowl so they warm through and absorb the flavours.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **Reverse Rotation**. Set **5 minutes / 90°C / Spoon speed**.
6.  **Serve:** Pour the cassoulet into a nice earthenware casserole dish, remove the bay leaf and thyme, and let it rest for 5 minutes before serving. Pure winter comfort!
