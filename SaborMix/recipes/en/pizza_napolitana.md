# Authentic Neapolitan Pizza (Dough and Sauce)
**Difficulty:** Medium | **Prep time:** 15 min | **Total time:** 24h (slow fermentation) or 2h (quick) | **Compatible with:** Essential and ProConnect.

*Achieve an elastic, airy dough worthy of the finest Neapolitan pizzeria, using the expert kneading function of your SaborMix.*

### Ingredients
**For the dough:**
*   500g bread flour (ideally type '00').
*   320g water.
*   3g fresh baker's yeast (or 1g dried yeast).
*   15g fine salt.
**For the topping:**
*   400g crushed tomatoes or *passata*.
*   2 balls of fresh mozzarella (drained).
*   Fresh basil leaves, extra virgin olive oil and salt.

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1.  **Activate the yeast:** Place the bowl on the SaborMix base. Add the 320g of water (using the integrated scale) and the fresh yeast.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **2 minutes / 37°C / Speed 2.**
2.  **Professional kneading:** Add the 500g of flour and the 15g of salt. Place the lid with the measuring cap.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* Select **Knead mode (Spike)** and set **4 minutes**. SaborMix will alternate rotations to develop the gluten perfectly.
3.  **Fermentation:** Remove the dough (it will be slightly sticky, this is normal) and shape into a ball.
    *   *If you have the Essential:* Place it in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth and leave to prove for 2 hours at room temperature (or 24h in the fridge for more flavour).
    *   *If you have the ProConnect:* Leave the dough in the bowl, select the **Fermentation mode** menu, set to **37°C for 1 hour and 30 minutes**. It will prove twice as fast in a controlled environment!
4.  **Quick tomato sauce:** Without washing the bowl thoroughly, add the crushed tomatoes, a drizzle of olive oil, salt and oregano.
    *   *SaborMix settings:* **5 minutes / 100°C / Speed 1**.
5.  **Baking:** Divide the dough into 3 or 4 balls, stretch them by hand from the centre outwards. Add the tomato sauce, bake at the maximum temperature of your oven (250°C or higher) for 6-8 minutes. Add the mozzarella and basil in the last minute.
